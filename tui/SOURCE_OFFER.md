# Written offer for corresponding source

This offer applies to the native nujinagent TUI executable distributed in the same release archive.

For at least three years after the release date, HKUDS offers any third party a complete machine-readable copy of the corresponding source and relinking materials required for the LGPL-covered libraries incorporated through Bun, for no more than the reasonable physical cost of conveying that copy.

The nujinagent application source, lockfile, build scripts, notices, and relinking instructions are already included in this archive. Exact upstream revisions are listed in `RELINKING.md`.

To request any additional corresponding-source material, open an issue at <https://github.com/HKUDS/nujinagent/issues> or email <xubinrencs@gmail.com> and identify the nujinagent release tag and target platform shown in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt`.
